# Privacy Policy & Ethical Standards

MailRadar is designed from the ground up for ethical open-source developer outreach. It is explicitly **not** a bulk harvesting or spam tool.

---

## 1. Principles of Ethical Outreach

1. **Explicitly Published Channels Only**:
   MailRadar only inspects public developer channels (README, CONTRIBUTING guides, official project documentation, and official project websites) where maintainers have publicly posted contact channels for collaboration, security, or project coordination.

2. **No Personal or Private Harvesting**:
   MailRadar strictly prohibits harvesting private personal Gmail/Yahoo accounts that lack official project maintainer context, private family details, personal financial data, or residential info. CAPTCHAs, authenticated firewalls, and technical bypasses are never used.

3. **Strict Attribution & Provenance**:
   Every contact stored in MailRadar includes full provenance: the exact source URL, context snippet, and extraction timestamp.

4. **Honest Verification**:
   MailRadar tests DNS/MX record validity. We never claim that an MX record proves individual mailbox presence, and we do not conduct aggressive SMTP dictionary attacks.

5. **Consent & Suppression Enforcement**:
   - The existence of a published email address is never treated as consent to receive mass unsolicited marketing.
   - Any opt-out, unsubscribe, or negative reply instantly suppresses the recipient across the entire organization.
   - Suppressed contacts are permanently barred from subsequent discovery campaigns.
   - Every outgoing message includes clear sender identity and functional unsubscribe headers.
